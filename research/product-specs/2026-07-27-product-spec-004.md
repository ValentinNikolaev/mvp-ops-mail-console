## Executive Summary

Incident Brief Generator

## Pros & Benefits

- Focuses the MVP on a concrete agency/MSP workflow: convert messy client delivery incidents into a defensible evidence brief, safe next action, and escalation packet.
- Keeps the reusable product spine from iteration 004: authentication, reputation, provider verdicts, placement, content, list health, cadence, and measurement remain separate evidence surfaces.
- Reduces false certainty by requiring every hypothesis to show observed evidence, missing evidence, contradictory evidence, not-observable states, confidence, and action risk.
- Builds value before fragile integrations: manual artifact intake, deterministic parsing/rules, and packet generation are enough for design-partner validation.
- Creates retention through incident history, reusable packets, recheck outcomes, and client-facing credibility for agencies/MSPs managing repeated deliverability problems.

## Cons & Risks

- Manual evidence can be incomplete, malformed, or selectively provided; the product must refuse unsupported ranking when minimum evidence is missing.
- Agencies/MSPs may want multi-client portfolio features early, but overbuilding account management before the incident loop works will slow validation.
- Provider opacity limits certainty; recommendations must separate observed fact, inference, and suggested action to avoid liability and reputation harm.
- Sensitive email artifacts require redaction previews, tenant isolation, access logs, short-lived raw file retention, and clear export controls from day one.
- A narrow first incident template may feel less ambitious, but a broad console would likely dilute urgency and make the evidence model harder to prove.

## Proposed Tech Stack & Tools

- Frontend: **Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS** — fast full-stack delivery for guided intake, evidence review, hypothesis panels, and packet preview in one codebase.
- Backend: **Next.js Route Handlers + Zod service layer** — typed request validation and a small deterministic rules API without premature service decomposition.
- Database: **PostgreSQL with Prisma** — durable multi-tenant case, evidence, hypothesis, packet, audit, and recheck models; JSONB stores parsed provider fields without blocking schema evolution.
- Queue or async jobs: **Inngest or Trigger.dev** — retryable parsing, evidence normalization, packet rendering, scheduled rechecks, and future connector pulls with low operational overhead.
- File storage: **Cloudflare R2 or S3** — encrypted raw headers, NDRs, screenshots, ESP CSVs, and support replies with lifecycle deletion; store redacted extracted facts in Postgres.
- Hosting: **Vercel + managed Postgres + managed object storage** — low idle cost, preview deployments, simple operations, and enough scale for design-partner traffic.
- Auth: **Clerk Organizations or Supabase Auth** — agency/client workspaces, invitations, RBAC, MFA-ready access, and fast implementation.
- Observability: **Sentry + structured JSON logs + Postgres audit table** — trace parser/rule failures, record decision provenance, and support incident replay.
- Release tooling: **GitHub CLI or GitHub Releases API** — publish `mvp-iteration-NNN` releases after MVP, Council verdict, and product spec artifacts are pushed to `main`.

### MVP Architecture

- Browser UI provides guided agency/MSP incident intake, artifact upload, redaction preview, evidence board, hypothesis panel, recheck checklist, and support-packet preview.
- Authenticated API validates case metadata, artifact manifests, tenant permissions, and manual observations.
- Object storage keeps raw artifacts with tenant-scoped keys and retention policy; Postgres keeps parsed/redacted evidence facts and audit events.
- Parser/normalizer jobs extract SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, SMTP/NDR codes, provider, sending IP/domain, Return-Path, DKIM domain, SCL/BCL when available, seed observation, ESP metrics, and operator notes.
- Deterministic rule engine maps evidence into hypotheses, missing-evidence prompts, contradictions, confidence, action risk, and safe next actions.
- Packet generator produces a redacted client/support brief with observed facts, inference boundaries, recommended action, and recheck plan.
- Release workflow publishes GitHub release `mvp-iteration-004` only after the MVP iteration, Council verdict, and product spec are committed and pushed.

### Product Scope

- First buyer: agencies/MSPs handling recurring client deliverability incidents.
- First incident template: client mail suddenly stops reaching Gmail/Outlook, lands in Spam/Promotions, or starts bouncing/rejecting after a sending, DNS, content, list, cadence, or ESP change.
- Case intake captures client/domain, sender identity, ESP, flow/campaign, provider impact, business impact, recent changes, headers, NDR/bounce text, ESP CSV, seed notes, screenshots, and support replies.
- Evidence quality gate determines whether the system can rank hypotheses, must ask for missing evidence, or should provide only a safe evidence checklist.
- Hypothesis output separates observed fact, inference, missing evidence, contradictory evidence, not-observable state, confidence, action risk, and verification step.
- Support packet export creates a redacted, shareable brief for ESP/provider/client escalation.
- Out of scope for this MVP: sending, warm-up automation, inbox guarantees, universal reputation score, deep provider integrations, and broad portfolio analytics.

### MVP Feature Set

- Guided incident file with plain questions: who rejected it, where did it land, what changed, what can be proved, and what safe next action is available?
- Artifact intake for headers, NDR/bounce text, ESP CSV/manual metrics, seed/placement observations, screenshots, and operator notes.
- Normalized evidence schema with provenance, freshness, owner, sensitivity, confidence contribution, contradictions, and recheck date.
- Minimum evidence rules for Gmail/Outlook rejection, bounce, Spam/Junk, Promotions, silent drop, and not-observable cases.
- Deterministic hypothesis rules for auth-pass delivery failure, provider-specific reputation, shared ESP/IP, content/list/cadence change, measurement-only anomaly, forwarding/path issue, and unknown.
- Confidence-gated recommendation panel that can refuse ranking and show the exact evidence needed next.
- Redaction preview and audit log before packet export.
- Recheck checklist that records outcome feedback without automatically treating correlation as proof.

### Fast, Cheap, Maintainable, Scalable Rationale

- A single TypeScript application keeps the build small and avoids operational drag while the evidence model is still changing.
- Manual imports avoid expensive and brittle provider integrations until design partners prove which connector retains users.
- Postgres plus JSONB supports structured evidence and raw parsed fragments without over-modeling every provider field early.
- Async jobs isolate parsing and packet generation failures from the interactive workflow.
- Deterministic rules are testable, inspectable, and safer for a trust-sensitive product than black-box recommendations.
- Tenant-scoped artifacts, redaction, and audit logs are easier to implement early than retrofit after sensitive customer data is flowing.
- The architecture can scale by adding connectors behind a stable `EvidenceSource` contract after the manual loop proves value.

### Operational Tradeoffs

- Choosing agencies/MSPs prioritizes repeated incident frequency and packet reuse over the fastest single-ESP import path.
- The first incident template remains narrow enough to ship but broad enough to test the reusable evidence schema across Gmail/Outlook placement and rejection symptoms.
- Refusing to rank weak-evidence cases may feel less magical, but it is necessary to build trust and avoid harmful remediation.
- A simple incident list replaces full portfolio analytics until case history and recheck data prove the retention loop.
- Outcome feedback improves future rule calibration, but must not silently increase confidence without reviewed evidence.

### Open Questions

- What minimum evidence should permit a ranked hypothesis for Gmail/Outlook placement, rejection, bounce, and not-observable incidents?
- Which design partners can provide 10-20 real agency/MSP client incidents with raw artifacts and known outcomes for evaluation?
- What packet format earns trust fastest: client-facing brief, ESP support ticket, provider escalation packet, or internal remediation memo?
- What pricing unit best matches agency/MSP value: client workspace, monitored domain, active incident, packet export, or seat?
- Which artifact type is hardest for users to obtain correctly: full headers, NDR text, ESP CSV, seed results, or support replies?
