Five replies agree that delivered/open metrics cannot prove Primary versus Promotions or Spam placement. They recommend combining seed tests, Google Postmaster reputation, engagement/list health, and conversion metrics rather than treating opens as placement evidence.

## Useful Comment Artifacts
- "Delivered" means accepted by the recipient server, not inbox placement.
- Apple MPP and provider differences make open rate an unreliable placement proxy.
